# Hospital-Emergency-Room-Dashboard-Power-BI

An *interactive Power BI dashboard* designed to explore hospital emergency room activity, monitor patient-flow indicators, and review referral and waiting-time patterns.

## **Dashboard Overview**

The report contains **four pages:**

- **Monthly View** — review emergency room activity over time.
- **Consolidated View** — explore an overall summary of key emergency room indicators.
- **Patient Details** — inspect patient-level details and use drill-through from department referral categories.
- **Key Takeaways** — present high-level findings and summary insights.

## **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

The dashboard highlights core emergency room metrics, including:

- **Total Patients** — *overall number of patient records/visits* represented in the dataset.
- **Average Wait Time** — *average time patients wait* before being seen.
- **Patient Satisfaction Score** — *patient satisfaction measure* available in the dataset.
- **Patients Referred** — *number of patients referred* to a department or service.

## **Analysis Included**

Depending on the selected page and filters, the report supports analysis of:

- Monthly patient volume and overall emergency room activity
- Admitted versus non-admitted patients
- Patient age groups (10-year bands)
- Patient distribution by gender
- Department referrals
- Percentage of patients seen within **30 minutes**
- Patient volume by day of week and hour
- Patient-level details through *drill-through navigation*

## **Tools and Skills**

- **Microsoft Power BI** — report design, interactive visuals, KPI cards, slicers, and drill-through
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **Data Modelling and Visual Analytics** — organizing data and presenting operational indicators
- **DAX** — calculated measures and categories, where used in the report
